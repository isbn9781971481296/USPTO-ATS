⚠️ Adjudicator Liabilities & Warnings:

All candidates, including the selected winner, are highly unlikely to succeed and will result in the irrevocable abandonment of the application. The candidates attempt to apply a $450 unapplied credit toward a $1,358 Extension of Time fee and request a Notice of Fee Deficiency under 37 CFR 1.53(f) for the remaining balance. Under 37 CFR 1.136(a), an extension of time is only granted if the petition is accompanied by the full fee. A Notice of Fee Deficiency cannot be used to toll the period for paying an extension fee to avoid abandonment.
