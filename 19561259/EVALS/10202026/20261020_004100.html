ATS Defect & Waterfall Alignment: The candidate aligns with the ATS brief's core requirement to cure pre-examination formalities and preemptively files a Substitute Specification to fix embedded drawings and claims. It correctly notes that all statutory fees were paid on October 15, 2026. ⚠️ Adjudicator Liabilities & Warnings:

Furthermore, it fails to execute the mandatory Path B directive to apply the $416.00 unapplied credits to specific fees, or alternatively, to request a refund under 37 CFR 1.26 as recommended in the ATS brief. This leaves $416.00 stranded in the Office of Finance. Despite this administrative oversight, the document is legally sufficient to advance the application to examination.
