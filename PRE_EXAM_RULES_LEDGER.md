## 1. Executive Directive: Pre-Emptive Rule Compliance & Universal Small Entity Status

1. **Universal Small Entity Mandate (37 CFR § 1.27):** ALL applications across the entire master portfolio MUST be prosecuted strictly under **Small Entity status** (37 CFR § 1.27). Never file or rely upon Micro Entity status (Form PTO/SB/15A is inapplicable due to the 4-application statutory cap under 35 U.S.C. § 123(a)(2)), and never default to undiscounted rates. Every filing must explicitly reaffirm Small Entity status, and all fees must be calculated using the Small Entity schedule.
2. **Pre-Emptive Rule Standard:** As notices are processed across the portfolio, any requirement cited by an examiner or petitions officer for a specific document type becomes a **mandatory, universal standard** for all subsequent filings across all applications—even if a particular examiner did not explicitly flag it. This eliminates cyclical notices, reduces abandonment risk, and saves progressive extension fees.
3. **Pure Statutory Headings (37 CFR § 1.72(b), § 1.75(h), § 1.125):** Technical documents that form part of the application disclosure (Claims, Abstract, Substitute Specification) must carry only pure, standard statutory headings. Never include defensive procedural parentheticals (such as `(COMMENCING ON A SEPARATE SHEET)`, `(WITH COMPLIANT LINE SPACING)`, or `(EXCISING EMBEDDED DRAWINGS)`) on technical sheets that enter the Pre-Grant Publication (PG-Pub) stream.

## 3. Comprehensive Non-Contradiction Audit

An exhaustive logical audit was conducted across Rules **R-01 through R-37** and Emergency Directive **D-10102026** to ensure zero conflicting requirements:

1. **Fee Remittance (R-06, R-10, R-12, R-16 vs D-10102026): PASS**
   - *Audit:* R-06 prohibits claiming non-existent deposit accounts. R-12 mandates Small Entity rate calculations. R-10 sets the $60 suspension fee (Fee Code 2830). R-16 and D-10102026 provide the emergency outage protocol: during portal payment outages, papers are filed on time without point-of-sale payment, requesting a formal Notice of Fee Deficiency under 37 CFR § 1.53(f) without making premature promissory undertakings.
   - *Verdict:* Completely harmonious. No conflict exists.

2. **Suspension of Action vs Pre-Exam Processing (R-07, R-08 vs R-22): PASS**
   - *Audit:* R-07 bars granting 1.103(a) suspensions while missing parts are pending, and R-08 states petitions do not toll reply periods. R-22 requires 1.136(a) extension petitions to prevent abandonment.
   - *Verdict:* Completely harmonious. The response package simultaneously cures all formalities and pays/requests extension under R-22, while structuring the suspension as a **Conditional Request** to take effect *upon* pre-examination clearance into TC 2100.

3. **Drawing Excision vs Specification Integrity (R-05, R-14 vs R-21): PASS**
   - *Audit:* R-14 requires excising embedded drawings from the specification. R-05 requires certifying no new matter under 37 CFR § 1.125(b). R-21 requires the Brief Description of Drawings to correspond with the drawing figures.
   - *Verdict:* Completely harmonious. Excising embedded graphical figures into standalone drawing sheets under 37 CFR § 1.84 satisfies 37 CFR § 1.58(a) without altering the technical text. The Brief Description in the Substitute Specification corresponds 1:1 with FIG. 1, FIG. 2, and FIG. 3.

4. **Technical Headings vs Separate Sheet Requirement (R-02, R-03 vs R-13): PASS**
   - *Audit:* R-02 and R-03 require Claims and Abstract to commence on separate physical sheets. R-13 mandates pure statutory headings without defensive procedural parentheticals (e.g., `(COMMENCING ON A SEPARATE SHEET)`).
   - *Verdict:* Completely harmonious. Physical separation is achieved via `\newpage`, while the heading remains pure and statutory (`CLAIMS` / `What is claimed is:` and `ABSTRACT OF THE DISCLOSURE`), avoiding publication contamination.

5. **Consecutive Claim Numbering (R-03 vs R-15): PASS**
   - *Audit:* R-03 mandates claims commencing on a separate sheet; R-15 mandates consecutive Arabic numbering (Claims 1 to 10) without duplicates or gaps under 37 CFR § 1.126.
   - *Verdict:* Completely harmonious.

6. **Fee Allocation Waterfall vs Outage & Deficiency Rules (R-06, R-16, R-24 vs R-25): PASS**
   - *Audit:* R-06 bans unauthorized deposit account assertions; R-16 and D-10102026 manage payment gateway outages via 37 CFR § 1.53(f) deficiency requests; R-24 contextualizes portfolio-wide detained capital ($32.7k-$42k); R-25 binds OPAP to debit unapplied funds for Extension fees (Priority 1) and Suspension fees (Priority 2) before base fees.
   - *Verdict:* Completely harmonious. Satisfying Extension fees first guarantees statutory timeliness under 35 U.S.C. § 133, while unpaid base fees are legally converted into extendable administrative deficiency windows under 37 CFR § 1.53(f) and MPEP § 506.

## 4. Universal Compliance Checklist for Portfolio Applications

Before generating documents for any application, verify against this unified checklist:
- [ ] **Universal Small Entity Status (R-12):** Explicitly reaffirm Small Entity status under 37 CFR § 1.27 in the response title and text. Never use Micro Entity forms or undiscounted fee codes.
