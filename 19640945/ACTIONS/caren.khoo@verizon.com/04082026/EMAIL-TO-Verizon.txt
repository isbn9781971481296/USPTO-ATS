Dear Vandana, Caren, Sanjeev, Mary, Jonathan, David, Brian, and Amar,
This letter serves as formal notice that a comprehensive portfolio of fifty patent applications has been released as a defensive
publication to establish definitive prior art in the fields of cellular, satellite, and RF communications. This technical record,
titled "Be Water, Be Light: Portfolio of Patent Applications Submitted to the USPTO" (ISBN: 978-1-97-148129-6), has been
accepted for publication in the International Journal of Law, Ethics, and Technology.

1. Anchors for Technical Disclosure
The following patent applications, formally filed with the USPTO between March 6 and March 9, 2026, serve as the technical
anchors for this notice:
USPTO # 19/640,945: Asynchronous Secure Communication

USPTO # 19/639,255: A High-Radix and Parametric Cellular Communication System.

USPTO # 19/639,265: A High-Radix and Parametric Satellite Communication System.

USPTO # 19/639,258: A High-Radix and Parametric Point-to-Point RF Communication System.

USPTO # 19/639,272: An Adaptive High-Radix and Parametric Troposcatter Communication System.
These inventions define a new architecture for optical computing and massively parallel data transmission, overcoming
fundamental bandwidth limitations of current serial/binary electronic systems.

2. Formal Notification to the European Patent Office (EPO)

On April 7, 2026, a formal notification was delivered to the European Patent Office (EPO), specifically addressed to Vice
President Rowan and Director Philpott of Directorate-General 1 (Patent Granting Process).
I have formally requested that this 721-page specification be indexed within the EPO's internal databases as Non-Patent
Literature (NPL). This ensures that examiners within Technical Clusters G06F and H04L cite this work as prior art during the
examination of any applications related to optical buses, high-radix computing, or satellite-to-ground communications.

3. Strategic National Security Status and U.S. Government License

Recognizing the foundational nature of these inventions for quantum computing clusters and national defense, I have granted
a perpetual, worldwide, no-fee license to the United States Federal Government for all governmental and defense
purposes.
