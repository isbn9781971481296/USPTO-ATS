\vspace{0.4cm}
\noindent What is claimed is:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, leftmargin=0.5in, itemsep=0.4cm]
    \item A method for secure communication between a sender and a receiver, comprising the steps of:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.3in, noitemsep]
        \item at the sender, generating a first secret key, $K_1$, which is not transmitted to the receiver;
        \item at the sender, encrypting a message $M$ using $K_1$ to produce an encrypted message;
        \item transmitting the encrypted message from the sender to the receiver;
        \item transmitting a prefix data block $P$ from the sender to the receiver;
        \item transmitting a suffix data block $S$ from the sender to the receiver;
        \item at the sender, deriving a second key, $K_2$, from $K_1$ and the combination of $P$, the encrypted message, and $S$;
        \item transmitting $K_2$ from the sender to the receiver; and
        \item at the receiver, decrypting the combination of $P$, the encrypted message, and $S$ using $K_2$.
    \end{itemize}

    \item The method of claim 1, wherein the encrypted message, the prefix $P$, the suffix $S$, and the second key $K_2$ are transmitted at different points in time.

    \item The method of claim 1, wherein at least two of the transmissions from the group consisting of the encrypted message, the prefix $P$, the suffix $S$, and the second key $K_2$, are performed over different communication channels.

    \item The method of claim 1, wherein the step of deriving $K_2$ comprises computing a cryptographic hash of the concatenation of $P$, the encrypted message, and $S$, and using the hash and $K_1$ as inputs to a key derivation function.

    \item A system for secure communication, comprising:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.3in, noitemsep]
        \item a sender device configured to:
        \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.2in, noitemsep]
            \item generate a non-transmitted first key $K_1$;
            \item encrypt a message $M$ with $K_1$;
            \item transmit the encrypted $M$, a prefix $P$, and a suffix $S$ in separate transmissions;
            \item derive a second key $K_2$ from $K_1$ and the combination of $P$, $M$, and $S$; and
            \item transmit $K_2$;
        \end{itemize}
        \item a receiver device configured to:
        \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.2in, noitemsep]
            \item receive the encrypted $M$, $P$, $S$, and $K_2$;
            \item assemble the received $P$, $M$, and $S$; and
            \item decrypt the assembled data using $K_2$.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}

    \item The system of claim 5, wherein the sender device is configured to perform the transmissions asynchronously.
\end{enumerate}
