The original two-month statutory response period expired on 2026-09-09. A one-month extension expired on 2026-10-09. As of today's date (2026-10-18), the application is in the second month of extension. To prevent abandonment, Applicant MUST file a complete reply on or before 2026-11-09 accompanied by a petition for a 2-Month Extension of Time under 37 CFR 1.136(a) and the requisite Small Entity extension fee of $276.00 [Fee Code 2252]. 3. Counsel should remit the $520.00 net base balance plus $276.00 extension fee (total $796.00) and request full credit reconciliation in the transmittal letter. 4. ACTION PLAN: - Prepare and file 'Applicant Response to Pre-Exam Formalities Notice' enclosing a standalone replacement Abstract complying with 37 CFR 1.72(b) and 37 CFR 1.121. - Concurrently submit Form PTO/SB/22 petition for a two-month extension of time under 37 CFR 1.136(a). - Pay $796.00 total via Patent Center ($520.00 base balance + $276.00 extension fee) prior to 2026-11-09 to place the application in condition for examination.
