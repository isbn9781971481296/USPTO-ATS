Application 19/560,038 ('OPTICAL COMPUTING ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM') is currently undergoing pre-examination processing in the Office of Patent Application Processing (OPAP), with Examiner ADD2AFC assigned. The Office issued a Notice to File Missing Parts (NTC.MISS.PRT) on August 5, 2026 setting a two-month extendable deadline that expired on October 5, 2026. Because today's date is October 9, 2026, a petition for extension of time under 37 CFR 1.136(a) (1 month to November 5, 2026, $94 Small Entity fee under Fee Code 2251) is mandatory to avoid abandonment. CRITICAL FORMALITIES & DEFICIENCIES IDENTIFIED BY OPAP: 1. Non-standard & Unanticipated Directive: The pre-exam notice identified embedded drawings/flow diagrams on pages 6, 7, and 8 of the original specification contrary to 37 CFR 1.58(a), requiring complete removal and standalone submission under 37 CFR 1.84 and 1.121(d). 2. Specification Formatting: Full specification line spacing failed 37 CFR 1.52(b), requiring a substitute specification under 37 CFR 1.125 with compliant 1.5/double spacing and an inventor statement of no new matter. 3. Separate Sheets: Original claims and abstract did not commence on separate sheets, violating 37 CFR 1.75(h) and 37 CFR 1.72(b). 4. Fees and Entity Status: The application was filed without basic filing, search, examination, and surcharge fees, incurring an additional non-DOCX fee under 37 CFR 1.16(u) and a late surcharge under 37 CFR 1.16(f). Micro entity status was claimed without the required form PTO/SB/15A/B. PROSECUTION STATUS & APPLICANT SUBMISSIONS: On October 1, 2026, the Office of Petitions (Attorney Advisor CB8D7D7) dismissed Applicant's Rule 183 petition seeking waiver of pre-exam deadlines and suspension of action, noting that pre-exam processing cannot be deferred or suspended under 37 CFR 1.103. On October 9, 2026, Applicant took positive corrective action by filing a formal Assertion of Entitlement to Small Entity Status under 37 CFR 1.27 (SES.REQ.PC), which is confirmed and entered in the Image File Wrapper. Furthermore, applicant prepared a comprehensive cure package dated October 9, 2026 comprising: (1) a Complete Response to the Notice to File Missing Parts, (2) a Petition for One-Month Extension of Time under 37 CFR 1.136(a) with $94 fee authorization, (3) a compliant Substitute Specification under 37 CFR 1.125, (4) Replacement Claims on a separate sheet (Claims 1-10), (5) a Replacement Abstract on a separate sheet, and (6) a Conditional Request for Suspension of Action under 37 CFR 1.103(a) effective upon pre-exam clearance. All .tex draft response files correctly cite Application Number 19560038 without any numbering discrepancy. However, because the response documents (other than SES.REQ.PC) have not yet posted to the Image File Wrapper, their status is classified as PENDING_USPTO_ENTRY. Counsel must immediately confirm electronic receipt and verify docket entry before the November 5, 2026 one-month extended deadline.
