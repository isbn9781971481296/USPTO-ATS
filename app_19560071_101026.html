PROSECUTION BRIEF & STATUS REPORT (APPLICATION 19/560,071) 1. CURRENT DOCKET POSTURE & PROCEDURAL HISTORY: Application 19/560,071 was filed on March 8, 2026, and is currently in pre-examination processing (Status Code 19). On July 9, 2026, the Office of Data Management mailed a Notice to File Missing Parts (NTC.MISS.PRT) setting a two-month response deadline of September 9, 2026. Deficiencies identified included: (a) claims not commencing on a separate sheet (37 CFR 1.75(h), 1.121); (b) abstract not commencing on a separate sheet (37 CFR 1.72(b), 1.121); (c) missing statutory filing, search, and examination fees, together with late surcharge (37 CFR 1.16(f)) and non-DOCX fee (37 CFR 1.16(u)); (d) unexecuted inventor oath/declaration; and (e) failure to establish micro entity status. 2. INTERVENING SUBMISSIONS & PETITION DISMISSAL: On July 26, 2026, Applicant submitted an executed inventor declaration under 37 CFR 1.63 (OATH) and an extensive Information Disclosure Statement with non-patent literature. On August 2, 2026, Applicant filed a verified Assertion of Entitlement to Small Entity Status under 37 CFR 1.27 (SES.REQ.PC), a Request for Early Publication (EARLYPUB), and a 108-page Petition under 37 CFR 1.183 (PET.OP) requesting suspension of prosecution on constitutional and national defense grounds, waiver of pre-exam deadlines, and reset of the notice commencement date. On October 5, 2026, the Office of Petitions mailed a Decision on Petition (PETDEC) DISMISSING the 37 CFR 1.183 petition. Crucially, the decision reiterated that under 37 CFR 1.181(f), the filing of a petition does NOT stay the time period for reply to an outstanding Office communication. The petition decision provided two months (until December 7, 2026) to file an optional Request for Reconsideration under 37 CFR 1.183, but explicitly warned that failure to timely reply to outstanding Office communications with requisite extension fees will result in abandonment under 37 CFR 1.53(f). 3. CRITICAL DEADLINE AUDIT & EXTENSION OF TIME REQUIRED: Because the 37 CFR 1.183 petition did not stay the clock, the original statutory two-month deadline to respond to the Notice to File Missing Parts expired on September 9, 2026. The one-month extension under 37 CFR 1.136(a) expired yesterday, October 9, 2026. As of today, October 10, 2026, the application requires a TWO-MONTH EXTENSION OF TIME under 37 CFR 1.136(a) (Fee Code 2252, $276.00 Small Entity) to extend the response deadline to November 9, 2026. The absolute maximum statutory five-month extension deadline is January 9, 2027. 4. MANDATORY CURES & SMALL ENTITY FEE RECONCILIATION: To avert procedural abandonment, counsel must immediately prepare and file a formal 'Applicant Response to Pre-Exam Formalities Notice' (PEFR) incorporating: a. Replacement Claims commencing on a separate sheet (37 CFR 1.75(h), 1.121, 1.126); b. Replacement Abstract commencing on a separate sheet (37 CFR 1.72(b), 1.121); c. Petition for Two-Month Extension of Time under 37 CFR 1.136(a) with $276.00 fee (Code 2252); d. Re-calculated Small Entity Statutory Filing Fees: Basic filing fee ($140), Search fee ($308), Examination fee ($352), Late surcharge under 1.16(f) ($68), and Non-DOCX penalty fee under 1.16(u) ($215), totaling $1,083.00, less the credited unapplied payment of $(416.00), yielding a net balance due of $667.00.
