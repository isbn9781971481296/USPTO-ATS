Submitting an executed micro entity certification will reduce fees by 80%.

4. STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION:
Given the immediate October 13, 2026 deadline, counsel must urgently finalize and submit the response under document description 'Applicant response to Pre-Exam Formalities Notice' along with the substitute specification, formal drawings, replacement claim listing, separate abstract, Micro Entity Certification, and balance of fees.
