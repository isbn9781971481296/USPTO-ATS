As of today (October 11, 2026), four months of extension have elapsed (October 8, 2026). The docket is currently in Month 5 of extension. To avoid abandonment, applicant MUST submit by November 9, 2026: (a) The remaining statutory fee balance of $486.00; and (b) An Extension of Time Petition under 37 CFR 1.136(a) for the 5th month (Fee Code 2255, Small Entity rate $1,358.00; or net payment of $1,082.00 after crediting the $276 previously paid on July 31, 2026). 4. WORKSHEET / NOTICE DISCREPANCY NOTE: A formal accounting conflict exists between the Fee Worksheet (WFEE / Form SB06) and the Notice of Incomplete Reply.
