​I.​​IBM​​Corporation:​​The​​"Quantum-Centric​
​Supercomputing"​​Blueprint​
​Target​​Operations:​​IBM​​Quantum,​​Qiskit​​v2.1,​​and​​the​​QCSC​​Reference​​Architecture​
​On​​March​​12,​​2026,​​IBM​​published​​a​​comprehensive​​blueprint​​detailing​​the​​integration​​of​​QPUs​​into​
​modern​​supercomputing​​environments,​​explicitly​​showcasing​​how​​quantum​​hardware​​operates​
​alongside​​co-located​​CPU​​and​​GPU​​clusters​​connected​​via​​low-latency​​interconnects​​(e.g.,​​NVQLink,​
​RDMA).​​This​​exact​​blueprint​​is​​legally​​encumbered​​by​​our​​portfolio.​
​●​ ​A​​Physically​​Disaggregated​​Computing​​System​​(USPTO​​19/562,845)​​&​​System​​for​​a​​Data​
​Center​​Architecture​​with​​Functionally​​Segregated​​Resource​​Pools​​(USPTO​​19/562,855):​
​IBM's​​multi-level​​hardware​​infrastructure​​specifically​​relies​​on​​isolating​​the​​quantum​​system​
​(which​​requires​​cryogenic​​cooling​​and​​specialized​​ASICs​​for​​error​​correction)​​from​​the​
​"partner​​scale-up"​​and​​"scale-out"​​classical​​GPU/CPU​​clusters.​​Our​​patents​​explicitly​​claim​​this​
​architecture:​​a​​computing​​system​​where​​primary​​components​​are​​physically​​disaggregated​
​into​​separate​​enclosures,​​functionally​​segregated​​by​​specialized​​infrastructure​​zones,​​and​
​interconnected​​by​​an​​ultra-high-bandwidth​​data​​fabric.​​1​
​●​ ​Method​​for​​Dynamic​​Composition​​of​​Bare-Metal​​Computing​​Resources​​(USPTO​
​19/562,853):​​IBM's​​system​​orchestration​​layer​​utilizes​​tools​​like​​the​​Quantum​​Resource​
​Management​​Interface​​(QRMI)​​and​​Slurm​​plugins​​to​​expose​​quantum​​resources​​as​​schedulable​
​entities.​​This​​directly​​infringes​​upon​​our​​method​​for​​dynamically​​composing​​a​​logical,​
​bare-metal​​computer​​from​​physically​​disaggregated​​pools​​of​​hardware​​resources​​on​​demand.​​1​

​License​
​Both​​IBM​​and​​Microsoft​​are​​primary​​contractors​​for​​the​​United​​States​​Department​​of​​Defense,​​the​
​Department​​of​​Energy,​​and​​the​​intelligence​​community.​​Microsoft's​​recent​​"Capital​​of​​Quantum"​
​initiative​​specifically​​highlights​​its​​proximity​​to​​and​​collaboration​​with​​the​​NSA.​
​You​​must​​be​​advised​​that​​within​​the​​legal​​text​​of​​these​​patent​​applications,​​the​​inventor​​has​​executed​
​an​​irrevocable,​​perpetual,​​worldwide,​​no-fee​​license​​granting​​the​​United​​States​​Federal​​Government​
​the​​right​​to​​utilize​​these​​inventions​​for​​all​​national​​defense​​purposes.​​1​ ​Consequently,​​while​​the​​U.S.​
​government​​is​​immune​​from​​infringement​​under​​28​​U.S.C.​​§​​1498,​​your​​unauthorized​
​commercialization​​of​​these​​architectures​​for​​private​​enterprise​​cloud​​offerings​​(e.g.,​​commercial​
​Azure​​or​​IBM​​Cloud​​clients)​​constitutes​​unshielded​​infringement.​
