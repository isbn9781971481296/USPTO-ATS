TO THE HONORABLE OSCAR TELFAIR, III, PRESIDING JUDGE:
Relator, REINALDO J. AGUIAR MARCANO, files this Fourth Mandatory Motion for De
Novo Hearing.
The Court held a hearing on September 11, 2025, which resulted in verbal orders that would
eventually be distorted and memorialized in the void ”Temporary Orders” (Index #89). However,
the draft for that order was not filed until many days later, and the final order was not signed until
October 15, 2025. This inordinate and unexplained delay between the hearing and the signing of
the order deprived Relator of his statutory three-day window under Texas Family Code §201.015
to request a de novo review of the September 11th proceedings.
Because Relator was procedurally prevented from exercising his mandatory right in a timely
fashion, the right is preserved. Relator hereby invokes his right to a de novo hearing for all matters
addressed in the September 11, 2025 hearing.
This Court’s refusal to rule on this and other mandatory motions constitutes an abdication of
judicial duty and is the subject of proceedings before the Supreme Court of Texas.
