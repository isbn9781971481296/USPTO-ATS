MOTION TO MODIFY TEMPORARY ORDERS
This Motion to Modify Temporary Orders is brought by Gloria Espina, Petitioner, who
shows in support:
1.

Since entry of the Temporary Orders, financial circumstances have materially

changed, which change requires the modification of the Temporary Orders. Gloria Espina requests
that the Temporary Orders be modified for the following reasons: Respondent’s refusal to pay
spousal support necessitates a change in possession of the marital residence.
Petitioner requests the Court award her the exclusive use and possession of the Valleyside
house, a judgment for outstanding spousal support, and a concurrent reduction in future spousal
support if the house is awarded to Petitioner.
Petitioner further requests that Respondent be ordered to pay interim attorney’s fees and
costs.
