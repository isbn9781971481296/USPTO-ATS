IL. STANDARD FOR INJUNCTIVE RELIEF

3. A party seeking a TRO must establish: (1) a substantial likelihood of success on the
merits; (2) a substantial threat of irreparable harm; (3) that the balance of hardships weighs
in their favor; and (4) that the issuance of the injunction will not disserve the public interest.

Winter v. Nat. Res. Dev. Council, Inc., 555 U.S. 7, 20 (2008).
