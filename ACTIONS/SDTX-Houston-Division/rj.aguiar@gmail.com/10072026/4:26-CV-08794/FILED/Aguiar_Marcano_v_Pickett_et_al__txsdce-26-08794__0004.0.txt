Counsel shall file with the clerk within fifteen days from receipt of this order a

initiative.

file not less than 10 days before the conference a joint discovery/case management plan
containing the information required on the attached form.

5.

conference.

Counsel who file or remove an action must serve a copy of this order with the summons
and complaint or with the notice of removal.
