CIVIL ACTION NO. 4:26-CV-08794

ORDER
Before the Court is the pro se plaintiff’s, Reinaldo J. Aguiar Marcano, emergency motion
for an ex parte preliminary injunction. The Court notes that the defendants have not been served
with citations and have not been served with this motion. The claim of the plaintiff is that he is
facing an imminent unconstitutional deprivation of physical liberty as he is faced with an arrest.
There is no evidence that the plaintiff sought relief in the Court where the matter against him
pending.
It is Ordered that the motion is Denied.
