NOTICE REGARDING CONSENT
TO JURISDICTION OF A MAGISTRATE JUDGE
This Notice is to remind Parties of their right to consent to the jurisdiction of a
Magistrate Judge to preside over all proceedings, including trials, and enter a final
judgment in the same manner as a District Judge. Consent to the jurisdiction of
Magistrate Judges improves the efficiency of the courts. The Judges of this Court
encourage parties to consent to the jurisdiction of Magistrate Judges pursuant to 28
U.S.C. § 636(c).
Upon all parties' consent, the case will be transferred to the Magistrate Judge
assigned to this case who will assume all the duties and powers of the District Judge,
including the power to preside over jury trials and enter final judgment.
All parties are free to consent or withhold consent to Magistrate Judge
jurisdiction without any adverse consequences.
Absent the parties' mutual consent to Magistrate Judge jurisdiction, District
Judges may still refer any or all pretrial matters to Magistrate Judges pursuant to 28
U.S.C. § 636(b).
If all parties choose to consent, please execute the attached Consent form, and
file it with the Clerk of Court. If all parties do not consent, no further action is
necessary.

Clerk's Notice Regarding Electronic Service
Important Information for People Representing Themselves Without a Lawyer
Receive Court Notices by Email
The United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas offers eligible
self-represented (pro se) litigants the option to receive automatic email notifications
(electronic service or e-service) when documents are filed in your case.
Consent to e-service is optional. If you do not consent to e-service, you will
continue to receive court notices and documents through the U.S. Mail.
Why Should I Consent to E-Service?
By consenting to e-service, you will receive Notice of Electronic Filing (NEF) emails
as soon as documents are filed in your case, allowing you to receive court notices
more quickly and avoid delays associated with postal mail. E-service helps you stay
informed about important activity in your case.
What to Know If You Consent to E-Service
If you choose to consent to e-service:
• You will no longer receive paper copies of court-filed or party-filed documents
in your case.
• You will receive a Notice of Electronic Filing (NEF) email whenever a docket
entry is made in your case. The NEF includes the case number, case
name/style, and the text of the docket entry.
• If a document is filed with that docket entry, the NEF will include a hyperlink
to the document.
• You may view each filed document one time without charge by using the
hyperlink in the NEF within 15 days after the notice is sent. You should
download or print the document the first time you view it.
• After your free view has been used or 15 days have passed, access to the
document requires a PACER account and applicable PACER fees may apply.
• Consenting to e-service does not authorize you to file documents
electronically. Separate approval from the presiding judge is required to
electronically file.
• You are still responsible for serving documents you file on the other parties in
your case as required by the applicable rules.
• You must promptly notify the Court if your email address changes.

E-Service Is Different From Electronic Filing
Receiving electronic notices does not authorize you to file documents electronically.
Unless the Court separately grants you permission to file documents electronically,
you must continue filing documents with the Clerk's Office in accordance with the
Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and the Local Rules of this Court.
Who May Consent
You may consent to e-service in your case if:
• you are representing yourself (you do not have an attorney representing you in
your case); and
• you are not currently incarcerated.
How to Consent to E-Service
Before consenting to e-service, be sure you have read this entire form to ensure you
are qualified and that you understand the effects of your consent. If you still want to
consent, complete and sign the attached Consent by Self-Represented Litigant to
Receive Notice of Electronic Filing (NEF) and return it to the Clerk's Office.
