\begin{center}
    \textbf{\large ABSTRACT OF THE DISCLOSURE}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.6cm}

A Central Processing Unit (CPU) architecture configured for the native storage and manipulation of numbers represented as parametric functions or symbolic recipes. The architecture comprises a set of parametric registers capable of holding a data structure that defines a number's mathematical formula, rather than its evaluated, fixed-width binary value. The CPU further includes a Parametric Logic Unit (PLU), which executes instructions on these registers by performing symbolic mathematical operations such as combining two parametric representations rather than traditional binary arithmetic. This design allows the CPU to perform calculations at a precision limited only by its internal logic, not by the fixed bit-width of its registers or data bus, eliminating the loss of precision that occurs when high-precision numbers are evaluated prematurely for processing.
