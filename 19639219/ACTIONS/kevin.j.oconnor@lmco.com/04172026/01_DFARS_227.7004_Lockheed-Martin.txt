I. THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE LICENSE GRANT
AND STRATEGIC BLOCKADE
Under standard operating procedures defined by 28 U.S.C. § 1498, the United States
Government exercises eminent domain over domestic patents, shielding its defense
contractors from civil injunctions and assuming financial liability for infringement.
However, the inventor of the Aguiar Marcano Intellectual Property Portfolio—which comprises
52 foundational patent applications published defensively in The Architecture of Control (ISBN
978-1-97-148129-6)—has proactively granted an irrevocable, perpetual, no-fee license to the
federal government for all national defense purposes. The United States Department of
Defense, specifically through Under Secretary of Defense Emil Michael, has been placed on
direct notice of this license grant and is the designated authority administering this license on
behalf of the military.
Lockheed Martin is currently utilizing these specific patented architectures to overcome severe
computational bottlenecks in their aerospace and quantum programs. By embedding the
research, development, and licensing costs of these technologies into multi-billion-dollar
government contracts, Lockheed Martin is effectively charging the American taxpayer for
intellectual property that the government already possesses the legal right to utilize indefinitely
and without cost. Failing to halt the disbursement of funds to this contractor for pre-licensed
intellectual property constitutes a gross mismanagement of taxpayer dollars.
