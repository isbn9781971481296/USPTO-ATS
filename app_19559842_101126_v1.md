  - Gross Small Entity Fees: $1,040.00
  - Less Unapplied Payment Credit: -$520.00
  - True Net Balance Due: $520.00.

Counsel must submit an explicit Fee Transmittal to ensure proper credit allocation.

5. ACTION PLAN BEFORE OCTOBER 21, 2026:
