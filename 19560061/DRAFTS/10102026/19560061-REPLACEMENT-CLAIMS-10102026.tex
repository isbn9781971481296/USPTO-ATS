\vspace{0.4cm}
\noindent What is claimed is:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}, leftmargin=0.5in, itemsep=0.4cm]
    \item A data storage apparatus, comprising:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.3in, noitemsep]
        \item a non-volatile storage medium;
        \item a storage controller communicatively coupled to the storage medium; and
        \item a massively parallel optical interface, coupled to the storage controller, configured to serve as the primary data input/output port for the apparatus.
    \end{itemize}

    \item The apparatus of claim 1, wherein the optical interface comprises a two-dimensional array of photon emitters and a two-dimensional array of photon sensors.

    \item The apparatus of claim 1, wherein the optical interface replaces a serial electrical interface such as SATA, SAS, or NVMe.

    \item The apparatus of claim 1, wherein the apparatus is configured to transfer data via the optical interface as a sequence of light frames at a rate exceeding 100 gigabits per second.

    \item A storage system, comprising a host computer and at least one Optically-Attached Storage (OAS) apparatus according to claim 1, wherein the host computer and the OAS apparatus are connected via a massively parallel optical link.
\end{enumerate}
