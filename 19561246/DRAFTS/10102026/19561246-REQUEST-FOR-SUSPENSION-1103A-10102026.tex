\section*{I. CONDITIONAL NATURE OF REQUEST (MPEP \S{} 709)}

Applicant acknowledges that under MPEP \S{} 709, a suspension of action under 37 CFR \S{} 1.103(a) cannot be granted during pre-examination processing while a Notice to File Missing Parts or Notice of Incomplete Reply is outstanding. Accordingly, this request is expressly structured as a \textbf{CONDITIONAL REQUEST} to take effect automatically upon the Office of Patent Application Processing (OPAP) verifying that the accompanying response package has fully cured all pre-examination formalities and entering the application into Technology Center 2100.

\section*{II. SHOWING OF GOOD AND SUFFICIENT CAUSE}

Good and sufficient cause exists for the requested suspension under 37 CFR \S{} 1.103(a):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Active Federal Civil Rights and Constitutional Litigation:} \\
    Applicant is the sole plaintiff in active federal litigation currently pending before the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, Houston Division, Case No. 4:26-cv-08794 (assigned to Judge Kenneth M. Hoyt; Cause: 28:1983 Civil Rights, Nature of Suit: 440 Civil Rights).

    \item \textbf{Protection of Patent Rights and Preservation of Status Quo:} \\
    The federal action seeks emergency injunctive relief to restrain unlawful state-court proceedings that have severely impaired Applicant's physical liberty and ability to access commercial counsel. A six-month suspension will allow the federal court to rule on the pending application for temporary restraining order and preserve Applicant's intellectual property assets without forcing premature abandonment or uncoordinated prosecution.
\end{enumerate}
